This Privacy Policy sets out how the Company collects, uses, discloses and protects Personal Data in connection with your access to and use of the Business Management Platform (the "Platform"). It also explains your rights and how to contact us.

Definitions

"Personal Data" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. "Process" or "Processing" has the meaning given in applicable data protection law.

1. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We process the following categories of Personal Data: (a) identity and contact data (e.g., name, email, job title, company); (b) account credentials (e.g., username, hashed password); (c) technical and usage data (e.g., IP address, device identifiers, browser type, log files, timestamps and pages/actions visited); and (d) any other information you voluntarily provide when using the Platform.

2. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We Process Personal Data for the following purposes: (i) to provide, operate and maintain the Platform; (ii) to authenticate and manage user accounts; (iii) to communicate with users; (iv) to perform security monitoring, fraud detection and auditing; (v) to comply with legal obligations; and (vi) for our legitimate interests, including improving and personalising the Platform. Where required by law, we will rely on your consent for specific processing activities.

3. Recipients and Third-Party Processors

We may disclose Personal Data to: (a) service providers and vendors acting as processors (e.g., hosting, analytics, email delivery, IT support); (b) affiliates and other entities under common control; (c) law enforcement, regulators or courts where required by law or to protect our legal rights; and (d) parties involved in a corporate transaction (e.g., sale, merger or reorganisation). We require our processors to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures and to act only on our instructions.

4. International Data Transfers

Personal Data processed by the Company may be transferred to and stored in jurisdictions outside your country of residence. Such transfers will be made in accordance with applicable law and where appropriate, we will implement safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or rely on adequacy decisions.

5. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy and to comply with legal, tax or accounting requirements. Retention periods may vary by data type and purpose; contact us for specific retention details.

6. Security Measures

We maintain reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage. These measures are periodically reviewed and updated. No transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure; incidents should be reported promptly to the contact below.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, rectification or deletion of, restriction of or objection to processing of, or portability of, your Personal Data. You may also withdraw any consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise your rights, contact us as set out below. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal timeframes.

The Platform may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the service and collect analytics. You may control cookies via your browser settings; however certain features may not function if cookies are disabled.

9. Changes to this Policy
